Our story

From a UAE workforce partner to a regional group.

PRTC Group was established in the UAE in 2008 to support the construction sector with reliable and skilled manpower solutions.

Managed by experienced professionals, we provide skilled, semi-skilled and general personnel for a wide range of construction and technical projects across the UAE and GCC. Our workforce of more than 1,000 personnel includes blue-collar and white-collar staff selected according to client requirements and project needs.
