Privacy

Privacy policy.

How information submitted through this website is handled.

Information you provide

When you submit a manpower enquiry, contact form or job application, you may provide details such as your name, company, phone number, email address, project information, employment information and uploaded CV or résumé.

How information is used

PRTC Group uses information submitted through the website to respond to enquiries, review applications, communicate about relevant project or employment matters, and operate the website.

How forms are processed

Website enquiries and career applications are processed using Supabase infrastructure. Enquiry details are stored in the website database. Career applications are stored in the database and uploaded CVs are kept in private file storage with restricted administrator access.

Service providers

PRTC Group uses service providers that support operation of the website and its backend, including Supabase for database, authentication and file storage. Additional providers may be used for operational email notifications if enabled.

Access and security

Access to enquiry and recruitment records is restricted to approved administrators. CV files are not published as unrestricted public links. Technical access controls are used to limit public access to administrative records and private files.

Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to handle the relevant enquiry, recruitment process, operational requirement or record-keeping need. PRTC Group may remove records that are no longer required.

Website data

The website may use standard technical logs or analytics if enabled by the site operator. These can include browser, device and page-visit information used to understand website performance and usage.

Your questions

For privacy questions relating to information submitted through this website, contact info@prtcgroup.com.
